[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20985)

Consistency metrics can hide a failure mode: a medical Vision-Language Model (VLM) that gives the same answer with or without the image. We classify safety per sample and find that a large share of "consistent" predictions are image-invariant, so aggregate consistency overstates how much a model can be trusted in the clinic.
