VSF-Med is an early pilot that scores vulnerabilities in medical Vision-Language Models (VLMs) using text-prompt attack templates, imperceptible visual perturbations, and an eight-dimensional risk rubric. It established the initial paraphrase-vulnerability and attention-stability setup that matured into PSF-Med. The ISBI contribution was a pilot abstract, not a peer-reviewed full paper.
