This companion study examines prompt sensitivity in text-only medical language models. It shows that chain-of-thought prompting can backfire, degrading answers on some clinical prompts where it was expected to help, and connects prompt-induced instability in language-only models to the dissertation’s paraphrase-sensitivity theme.
