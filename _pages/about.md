    <h2 id="beliefs-title">My beliefs</h2>
    <p>We can help create a world we’re glad to live in.</p>

<p class="thoughts-hint">Hover or tap to read more.</p>
<ul class="thoughts-list">
  <li><details><summary>Build things that are useful to people.</summary><p>I care about where the work ends up. In healthcare, I want what I build to help someone make a better decision or make good care more accessible. That purpose helps me choose which problems are worth spending time on, even when the answer is a long way off.</p></details></li>
  <li><details><summary>Understand why something works.</summary><p>A good result makes me curious about what produced it. I want to understand the assumptions behind it and the conditions under which it fails. Finding a failure can be useful progress. It gives us something specific to investigate and a better question to ask next.</p></details></li>
  <li><details><summary>Make problems easy to talk about and be open to being wrong.</summary><p>I want to hear about a concern while there is still time to act, even if we cannot fully explain it yet. I try to thank people for raising it, so they feel free to speak up again. I hold my own thinking to the same standard and ask what would change my mind, especially when I like my answer. When the evidence points elsewhere, I follow it and explain what I got wrong. If I was confident in public, I correct myself there too.</p></details></li>
  <li><details><summary>Give people a clear goal and room to act.</summary><p>I want people to understand why the work matters and who is responsible for moving it forward. That responsibility should come with the authority to make decisions. I explain the constraints, offer honest feedback, and trust the person doing the work to choose how to approach it.</p></details></li>
</ul>
